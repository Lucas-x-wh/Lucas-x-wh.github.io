THE READING ROOM

The art of
paying attention

Reading slowly gives unfamiliar ideas time to take shape. A single paragraph can invite us to reconsider what we know, connect different experiences, and find a new perspective.

Attention is a habit we can develop. The goal is to leave the page with something that was not there when we began.

Original sample text

尽管证据有限，
这些发现仍然令人期待。
